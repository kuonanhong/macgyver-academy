## Method

1. **Terms.** English title n-grams (weight 1.0), Chinese title keywords via the zh table (0.8), proper nouns via the names table (1.0), sub-category (0.45), diagram hints (0.3/0.2), catalog category (0.3) and summary (0.25, synonym-table terms only). Generic words are down-weighted.
2. **Synonyms.** `tools/icons-v12/synonyms.json` maps 2566 English engineering terms, 547 proper nouns and 490 Chinese keywords to icon names/tokens, plus per-category theme lists and name-prefix families.
3. **Evidence.** Best target match per term (full name 1.0, name token 0.7, MDI alias 0.55, tag 0.3 capped at 0.6), diminishing returns across terms (x0.6 each), name-coverage factor 0.15–1.0; category theme bonus up to 0.30; badge variants (e.g. `airplane-cog`) inherit 30% of their base glyph's match.
4. **Selection.** Match evidence x glyph prior x 0.6 for MDI (an MDI glyph must match about 67% better than every unused Tabler glyph) + theme + inheritance. The 17 flagship courses (named ids) get a x2.2 boost so they pick first.
5. **Uniqueness.** Global best-first greedy over all (project, glyph, score) pairs, then exchange chains (depth ≤ 2) that raise the summed score, then category / neutral pools. Weak picks with neither concept nor theme evidence become neutral geometric glyphs rather than misleading pictograms.
6. **Policy.** Never: Tabler *Brand*, MDI *Brand / Logo*, product trademarks (e.g. Steam, PlayStation, Olympic rings, Apple devices, Death Star), religious, weapon and drug symbols. Last resort only: letters, numbers, zodiac, gender, mood, laundry, gestures, badges, `-off` variants, badge variants of UI glyphs and trivial glyphs.

## Why the tail is weak

Several concept clusters have far more projects than distinct glyphs exist for them (e.g. 58 tunnel titles, 42 bridge titles, 35 dam titles, 33 telescope titles, 56 aircraft / airliner titles, 27 wind titles, 18 hydropower titles). Once the on-concept glyphs are taken, the remaining members receive the best still-free themed glyph, a badge variant of their concept, or a neutral geometric shape (q close to 0).
