Notable accuracy decisions:
- a188 checks loss, endpoint/common-cause limits, and numerical degraded capacity;
  200 -> 100 Gb/s fails a 150 Gb/s minimum despite surviving connectivity.
- a190 labels the 6 dB budget as EXCESS loss and separately requires ideal splitting.
- a195 only simulates non-emitting interlock states. Its 4.5 ms scheduling bound
  is neither a safe exposure duration nor a laser-classification result. No
  proprietary or invented laser exposure limits are supplied.
- a196 separates CoWoS-S/R/L and calls all geometry assumptions hypothetical.
- a197 independence is an explicit yield assumption, not a vendor production yield.
- a198 distinguishes stored capacitor energy 4.9 fJ from supply energy 9.8 fJ.
  Intel's Foveros-R 2027 production-ready statement is explicitly future planning
  at this October 2026 review; all Foveros variants are not called hybrid bonds.
- a199 does not repeat the outdated blanket claim that all EMIB is TSV-free.
  A fixed 33.3 ps delay (using c approximately 3.00e8 m/s) is not random jitter.
- a200 distinguishes iSiPP200/iSiPP300, licensed PDK/run options and a hypothetical
  local FSR calculation; a public overview does not provide fabrication permission.
