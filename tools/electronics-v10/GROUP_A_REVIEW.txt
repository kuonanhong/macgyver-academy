Safety and certification scope: all HV/grid work is offline diagramming,
simulation, test planning and report interpretation. No live switching orders,
actual relay settings, energization SOP, installation approval or professional
license is implied. Course completion measures learning tasks, not permission
to construct or operate industrial equipment.

Limitations: the group contributes curriculum, links and representative maps;
it does not add actual industrial datasets or vendor factory access. Numerical
cases are deliberately hypothetical and media/interactive integration is handled
by the main V10 build. Commercial providers are lesson-role references, without
stock prices, endorsement or a claim that vendors teach these courses.
