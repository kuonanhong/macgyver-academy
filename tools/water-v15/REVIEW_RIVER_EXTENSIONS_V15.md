## Numerical and physical checks

Formulas do not imply a universal entrainment/filter criterion, basin length, rock stability or future knickpoint speed. Uplift inputs explicitly mean net upward pressure.

b278 checks the loss-free jet scale, opening discharge, pool power density, water-relative swimming, attraction allocation, nominal water turnover, screen opening velocity, eddy scale, detection correction and sequential conditional probabilities. Screen opening velocity is explicitly distinct from gross-area superficial approach velocity. Cumulative passage uses conditional stage probabilities; independence is only needed if unrelated marginal rates are substituted.

b279 checks consistent planform endpoints, geometric slope versus independently assumed energy slope, SI Manning roughness units, geometric floodplain capacity, bend scale, in-place bank volume, gross side-channel inflow, Darcy discharge, water advection and independent random terrain-error propagation. None substitutes for flood-peak reduction, exported sediment mass, pore velocity or a confidence interval.

b280 checks empirical probabilities and the stated quantile convention, a four-case Brier average, trapezoidal storage balance, already-routed junction arrivals, residual arrival allowance, incremental net prerelease time, a positive infeasibility gap, scenario violation frequency and relative MAE skill. It does not authorize releases, invent rule priority, equate ensemble frequency with true risk, or equate prediction skill with flood-risk reduction.
