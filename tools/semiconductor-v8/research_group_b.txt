AUTHORING
Every course has 24 individually written bilingual actions and 24 individually
written technical conclusions. Stage-specific principles and misconceptions
support the actions. Shared evidence formats specify diagrams, comparisons,
hypothetical calculations, causal reasoning, controls and acceptance reports.
Each quiz has 3 options and each course balances correct positions 8/8/8.
Previous-step prerequisites are explicit. Existing catalogue titles/IDs remain.
All text is provided in Traditional Chinese and English, not claimed to be a
human translation into all site UI languages.
