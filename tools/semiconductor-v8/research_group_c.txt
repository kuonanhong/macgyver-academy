Group C V8 authoring and review note
==================================

Scope: catalog IDs a067-a099 (33 titles preserved). Each course has four
stages of six steps: twelve distinct technical topics, each with a model/
analysis task and a diagnostic/verification task. The diagnostic step adds
a separate physical mechanism or measurement uncertainty. Every course
also contains one explicit numerical worksheet with assumptions, units,
calculation, result, limitations and an acceptance check.

Counts: 33 courses; 792 steps; 1,584 bilingual knowledge records; 33 worked
examples; 79 primary-source records; 33 place records using seven shared
representative locations. Related comparisons are independently authored
in group_c_related.json and merged by the release integrator.

Sources: the builder contains the full source registry and public URLs.
Primary manufacturer, research-institution, university, NIST and official
history/map pages were read during authoring. Public papers are summarized
only to the scope actually available. These are educational interpretations
of public principles, not disclosed production recipes or licensed manuals.
Each course links at least two technical references; some well-matched
references support several courses. Source links do not establish that
every instructor-created numerical example appeared in that source.

Specific limitations:
- Keysight param-guide is a public chapter/handbook overview; the gated
  complete handbook was not accessed.
- IEEE RRAM relaxation entry is supported by the public abstract only.
- All worked-example numbers are invented teaching inputs, never claimed
  to be fab data, OEM specifications or recommended physical settings.
- NICT vertical Ga2O3 research is not interpreted as proof of practical
  mobile-hole p-type conduction merely from an implantation description.
- MRAM endurance claims are not treated as proof of infinite endurance.
- No new photographs or videos are included in this group file; release
  media integration is handled separately. No third-party images were
  downloaded, copied or relicensed by this group.
- This content is complete in Traditional Chinese and English only.

Location precision and history:
- imec Leuven and NIST Gaithersburg reuse the reviewed V7 representative
  campus points; institution roles are distinguished from fabrication claims.
- Hamamatsu headquarters uses the official embedded-map DMS marker.
- Bosch Sensortec headquarters uses the official directions-page GPS point.
- NICT Koganei uses a NASA ILRS historical station point as an approximate
  campus reference, explicitly not the Ga2O3 laboratory position.
- Nagoya University's campus reference uses an official tourism page's
  embedded-map center, explicitly not the exact Akasaki Institute entrance.
- TSMC headquarters/Fab 12A reference uses the Morris Chang Building pin
  linked from the official fabs page, not utilities or equipment coordinates.
- Each location entry includes two sourced milestones. Shared campus
  histories provide geographical context, not claims that every course
  invention originated there. NanoIC opening, tool arrival, qualification
  and production readiness are kept as separate milestones.

- Checked prerequisite ordering, source-ID resolution, nonempty bilingual

- Checked unique instructions, 33 visible worked-example labels, valid map

- Reviewed worked-example unit conversions and arithmetic. Simplified
