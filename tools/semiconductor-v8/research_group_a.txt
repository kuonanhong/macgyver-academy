Research date: 2026-10-14 Asia/Taipei

Scope and output
- 29 new courses: a003..a033 except a004 and a025 (existing courses preserved by root).
- 4 stages x 6 steps each = 696 bilingual concept work packages.
- 1,392 knowledge entries: 696 individual topic mechanisms, plus 116 distinct
  stage-specific technical explanations reused within their relevant stage.
  Entry count is not a claim of 1,392 wholly distinct scientific principles.
- 29 explicit, independently authored worked exercises. All input numbers are
  labeled hypothetical; elementary models include kerf count, defect adders,
  mass balance, series capacitance, dose integration, diffusion, TLM, geometric
  contact landing, gate-gap budgets, transport tail latency and Poisson analogy.
- 43 primary source records, 29 representative-place entries, 58 directed
  related-course comparisons. No media binaries copied or licensed here.
- Six evidence-work formats are reused: diagram, comparison, sensitivity,
  diagnosis, measurement plan and acceptance. Domain mechanisms and all 696
  instructions differ. These are educational analysis tasks, not 696 unique
  physical simulators, manufacturing recipes or accredited lab protocols.

Sources inspected
Official parsed pages, supplied PDF text extracts and research abstracts were
read through web retrieval. The JSON records preserve original URLs and precise
scope. No claim is made that every full PDF or linked paywalled paper was read.

history. MicroChemicals: wafer production/specifications, oxidation/coatings,
implant masks, resist composition, spin coating, softbake, PEB, development and
antireflection application notes. These legacy/general notes are not presented
as proprietary advanced-node or DUV-specific recipes.

and 6.774 front-end physics overview. Original assignments and hypothetical
models here are not copied exercises or illustrations.

thermal processing, single-wafer front-end historical announcement, and glossary
strain-engineering entries. Historical releases are dated background, not current
supplier process guarantees.

etch application page. Marketing superlatives are not adopted as technical facts.

imec: 2019 FinFET/nanosheet/forksheet/CFET discussion, 2024 monolithic CFET
